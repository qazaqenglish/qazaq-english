READING

Kazakh Hospitality

Hospitality has always been an important part of Kazakh culture. Guests are traditionally welcomed with food, tea and warm conversation.

In Kazakh culture, welcoming a guest is often considered a sign of respect and generosity. Sharing food and conversation helps create strong relationships between people.

Reading question:

Why is hospitality important in Kazakh culture?
