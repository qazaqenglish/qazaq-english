READING 01

Kazakh Hospitality

Hospitality has always been an important part of Kazakh culture. For many generations, welcoming guests has been considered a sign of respect, kindness and generosity. In traditional Kazakh society, guests were often offered the best food available in the home.

When visitors arrived, families could prepare tea, bread, baursak, meat and other traditional foods. Conversation was also an important part of the visit. People talked about their families, journeys, news and everyday life.

Hospitality was especially important because people living on the steppe sometimes travelled long distances. A traveller could need food, water, warmth and a place to rest. Helping such a person was connected with important ideas about humanity and respect.

Questions

1. What does hospitality show?

2. Why could travellers need help?

READING 02

The Dombra and Kuy

The dombra is one of the best-known symbols of Kazakh musical culture. It is a traditional two-stringed instrument that has been used for generations. Its simple shape makes it easy to recognize, but its musical possibilities are rich.

One important musical tradition connected with the dombra is called kuy. A kuy is an instrumental musical composition that can tell a story or express feelings without using words.

Skilled musicians can use rhythm, melody and changes in sound to create different moods. Through music, listeners can learn about history, nature, people and important events.

Questions

1. How many strings does the dombra traditionally have?

2. What can a kuy express?

READING 03

Nauryz

Nauryz is an important spring celebration in Kazakhstan and many other countries of Central Asia. The holiday is connected with renewal, friendship, peace and the beginning of a new year according to the traditional calendar.

During Nauryz, families and communities come together. People visit relatives and friends, prepare traditional food and take part in public celebrations. Special dishes and drinks may be prepared for guests.

Nauryz also has a strong connection with nature. Spring represents new beginnings, and the celebration encourages people to think positively about the future.

Questions

1. What does Nauryz represent?

2. What do people often do during Nauryz?
